An accretion formed by powerful Volkodlak.

### material: material_112154

An accretion formed by powerful Volkodlak.
An accretion that has fully fused with an organ or bone. One could even say it's a newly grown bone or organ at the nodes of the Volkodlak's power circulation. Only long-lived Volkodlaks who have ceaselessly honed their strength can form such growths within their bodies.
