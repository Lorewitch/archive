## EN

### A Splash of Color on the Plate!

You have finished collecting the free-roaming color paints. The colorist Ashas are very pleased with the haul...

#### Talk to Bennett

**Bennett:** Looks like our colorist Ashas are very much satisfied now.

**Bennett:** Another challenge completed for our wondrous Ashas!

**Paimon:** Awesome! We did it!

**Paimon:** Is this Asha here inspired to start another artwork already?

- **Traveler:** Well, you know how inspiration can come and go in the blink of an eye...

- **Traveler:** So you have to strike while the iron is still hot.

**Bennett:** Ah, look at all the treasure it's left behind... Traveler and Paimon, the two of you should have it! For all the hard work you've put in!

**Bennett:** Maybe the next time we come by, we'll be able to see the colorist Asha's masterpiece.

**Paimon:** Just imagine what kind of work it would be! Especially when even the regular Ashas are already capable of coming up with such beautiful colors.

**Paimon:** Paimon can't wait to see what the colorist Ashas will create! Maybe they'll come up with something... beyond what words can describe.

**Paimon:** Maybe the colors of Phlogiston, the moon, or the depths of the starry sky?

**Bennett:** Yeah, that's exactly how it should be! If we come across a creature in some kind of trouble during our adventures, we should do everything we can to help it...

**Paimon:** Mm-hmm! Who knows? Maybe one day we'll receive some unexpected rewards!

- **Traveler:** I agree with Bennett.

- **Traveler:** And it feels very fulfilling as well.

**Bennett:** Ah, but... We shouldn't do it just because of some expected reward. That'd be beside the point.

**Bennett:** Even if there's nothing in it for us, helping friends in need and seeing their smiles return as things turn for the better...

**Bennett:** I really like that feeling!
