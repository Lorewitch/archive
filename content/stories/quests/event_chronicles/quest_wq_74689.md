### Hot in a Flash, or Cold Just as Fast?

It is said that Lepine-Pauline has developed a groundbreaking new lens. But why does her apprentice Ninette look on the verge of tears?

#### Talk to Ninette

**Ninette:** (...Could they be the ones Master mentioned? The golden-haired traveler, and that flying fairy dressed in white...)

**Paimon:** Huh? Why are you staring at us and muttering like that? Is there something weird on our faces?

**Ninette:** Ah! M—My apologies! I didn't mean to! I just... I just thought...

- **Traveler:** There's no rush. Please, take your time.

- **Traveler:** Do I know you?

**Ninette:** I... I'm Ninette, and my master is Lepine-Pauline. She told me about you... said you're the legendary adventurer Traveler, and that you travel with your best companion, Paimon!

**Ninette:** I... I never imagined I'd really get to see you in person...

**Paimon:** Oh! So you're Lepine-Pauline's apprentice! Guess her business has really been booming lately, huh?

**Ninette:** Yes! And it's all thanks to her that I was able to get back on my feet after a really hard time.

Did something happen?

**Ninette:** I used to study photography under another teacher.

**Ninette:** I wanted to make it on my own someday, so I spent all my savings on the best Kamera equipment I could afford.

**Ninette:** My teacher said my skills weren't bad, and I really believed that if I just took good photos, I'd be able to make a living.

**Ninette:** But... when I actually went out on the streets and saw people, I got so nervous that I couldn't get a single word out.

**Ninette:** Every time I tried to say, "Hello, would you like a photo?", the words just got stuck in my throat.

**Ninette:** In the end, everyone just went to the photographers who were friendlier and better at talking to people.

**Ninette:** Before I knew it, I'd almost run out of Mora... and I even thought about selling my Kamera just to get by...

That must've been really hard.

**Ninette:** It was right then that I met Master Lepine-Pauline.

**Ninette:** When she saw my equipment and the photos I'd taken, she encouraged me and had me work with her to test a new lens.

**Ninette:** She said there was a calm, gentle quality in my photos... something she didn't see in anyone else's.

**Ninette:** So when Master finally finished developing the Shutterflash lens, I was really happy! Probably even more than she was!

**Paimon:** The Shutterflash lens? What kind of lens is that?

**Ninette:** It captures every movement and expression perfectly, even when the subject's moving really fast!

**Ninette:** But... it's still in the final testing phase, so we need to take a lot of real-world photos to gather data and make sure it works properly.

**Ninette:** Since Master's been tied up with other work, she handed this task to me. I was thinking I could try finding a few people to help with the testing...

**Ninette:** But... it happened again. Whenever I tried to talk to someone I didn't know, I got all flustered and couldn't make any sense...

**Ninette:** Everyone just looked at me kind of confused... they probably thought I was acting suspicious or something. A few days went by, and I still couldn't find anyone willing to help.

**Ninette:** *sobbing*... I couldn't even handle something this easy... I guess I really am hopeless...

**Paimon:** Hey, hey, don't cry! It's really not your fault, okay?

**Paimon:** Really, asking strangers for help like that is hard for anyone!

- **Traveler:** Don't beat yourself up over it. You've already tried your best.

- **Traveler:** In that case... maybe we could lend a hand.

**Paimon:** Uh-huh! Traveler's got great photo skills! And he / she knows tons of people all over the place, so getting models should be super easy!

**Ninette:** R—Really? You'd actually help me?

**Ninette:** W—Whoa! Thank you so, so much, Traveler!

**Ninette:** Then... please take this special Kamera with the Shutterflash lens. I'll be counting on you to handle it!

**Ninette:** Please take some photos of your friends. Try to capture their most natural, expressive moments!

#### 与妮内特对话

**妮内特:** （…是他们 / 她们吗…师父说的…金发的旅人和会飞的白色精灵…）

**派蒙:** 嗯？你一直盯着我们看，还嘀嘀咕咕的，难道我们脸上有什么奇怪的东西吗？

**妮内特:** 呀！对、对不起！我不是故意的！我只是…只是觉得…

- **旅行者:** 别紧张，慢慢说。

- **旅行者:** 我们认识吗？

**妮内特:** 嗯…我叫妮内特，我的师父是乐平波琳。她、她提过您，说您是…传奇的冒险家旅行者，身边还有最好的伙伴派蒙！

**妮内特:** 我、我没想到真的能见到你们…

**派蒙:** 哦！原来是乐平波琳的徒弟啊！看来她最近事业发展得不错嘛。

**妮内特:** 嗯！而且，多亏了师父，我才能从人生低谷里爬起来。

发生什么事了？

**妮内特:** 我原本是跟着另一位老师学习摄影的。

**妮内特:** 为了能成为独当一面的摄影师，我把所有积蓄都投了进去，买了一整套当时最昂贵的留影设备。

**妮内特:** 我的技术，老师也说还不错…我以为，只要拍出好画片，就一定能赚到钱的。

**妮内特:** 可是…可是我一到街上，看到路人，就紧张得一个字都说不出来。

**妮内特:** 想问一句「您好，需要留影吗？」，话就在嘴边打转，怎么也开不了口。

**妮内特:** 结果，客人们全都跑去找那些能说会道、热情招揽的摄影师了。

**妮内特:** 后来，我连摩拉都快花光了，甚至都准备把心爱的留影机卖掉换点生活费了…

真是不容易啊。

**妮内特:** 就在那个时候，我遇到了乐平波琳师父。

**妮内特:** 她看到我的设备和画片之后，鼓励了我，还让我跟着她帮忙测试新镜头。

**妮内特:** 她说…她说我拍出的画片里，有一种别人没有的安静和温柔。

**妮内特:** 所以！当师父成功研发出这款「瞬影」镜头时，我比谁都高兴！

**派蒙:** 「瞬影」镜头？这是做什么用的？

**妮内特:** 它能清晰地捕捉到高速运动中的人物姿态和神韵！

**妮内特:** 不过…它目前还处在最终测试阶段，需要大量的实际拍摄来收集数据，验证性能。

**妮内特:** 师父最近分不开身，就把这个任务交给了我。我本来想着，可以去招募一些临时测试员来帮忙…

**妮内特:** 可是…可是我又犯了老毛病。每次主动去跟陌生人搭话，我就会紧张得语无伦次…

**妮内特:** 结果他们都用疑惑的眼神看着我，大概是把我当成什么可疑的人了…好几天过去了，还是没有一个人愿意帮我。

**妮内特:** 呜…我连这么简单的事情都办不好，我真是太没用了…

**派蒙:** 欸，别哭啊！这也不能怪你嘛！

**派蒙:** 突然跑去拜托陌生人帮忙，对很多人来说都是个挑战啦。

- **旅行者:** 别自责了，你已经很努力了。

- **旅行者:** 这件事，或许我们可以帮忙。

**派蒙:** 对啊！旅行者的拍摄技术可好了，而且，他 / 她的朋友遍布整个大陆，找人留影这事肯定没问题！

**妮内特:** 真、真的吗？您…您愿意帮我？

**妮内特:** 呜哇——！真的、真的太感谢您了！旅行者大人！

**妮内特:** 这台搭载了「瞬影」镜头的特制留影机就拜托您了！

**妮内特:** 请用它记录下您那些朋友们最生动、最独特的瞬间吧！
