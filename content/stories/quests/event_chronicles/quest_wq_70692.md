Thanks to recent exchanges with friends from different nations, Mika from the Reconnaissance Company has reached a new milestone...

#### Talk to Mika

**"Mural Maniac":** Wow, young Knight, looks like you've brought our great hero over!

**Mika:** It's our Honorary Knight and the Helper in White! Greetings!

**Paimon:** Mika and... a friend from Natlan? What are you guys doing here?

**Mika:** We're here to get the word out about something called the Saury-Saury Scout. We think it's something adventurers will find really useful.

**Mika:** It combines the mechanical core of the Scouting Buddy that Amber brought back from Sumeru with the adorable appearance of the little saurian toy my friends from Natlan gave me.

**Paimon:** Scouting Buddy... Oh, yeah, Paimon remembers!

**Mika:** Just like the Scouting Buddies from Sumeru, the Saury-Saury Scout can be used to perform monster recon and survey unfamiliar terrain.

**Mika:** But due to our focus being on the latter, the Saury-Saury Scout is designed with a greater emphasis on controllability and precision of movement.

**Mika:** ...Are you interested in taking it on a test flight, Honorary Knight?

- **Traveler:** That was a very serious introduction.

- **Traveler:** There's no need to be so formal.

**Mika:** Huh? Perhaps I was a little bit nervous just now...

**Mika:** That must be it... I could swear I was more relaxed around you before.

**Mika:** It's just that I've been hearing so many stories about you from friends in other nations lately — your deeds are getting more and more exceptional...

**Mika:** Every time I think about how I need to be helpful and not get in your way, I can't help but tense up again.

**Paimon:** That's such a Mika thing to say...

**"Mural Maniac":** You're doing very well, young Knight!

- **Traveler:** Let's see what this Saury-Saury can do!

**Mika:** Fantastic! Let me just make a few final tweaks, and then you're good to go!
