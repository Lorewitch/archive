### Странный отшельник

На севере острова Лемпо Девонт, отшельник, живущий в уединении на восточной стороне Пустынного коридора, раньше регулярно посещал Нашгород. Но в последнее время, не говоря уже о посещении города, он даже отказывается принимать гостей, которые приходят к нему. Он живёт уединённо. Похоже, теперь одевается в ином стиле и изменил свой образ жизни... Стоит отметить ещё один момент. Один странствующий торговец утверждал, что регулярно навещает отшельника, но его местонахождение неизвестно с момента последнего визита... Если вы хотите узнать правду, то, возможно, лучшим вариантом будет личное посещение.

#### Отправляйтесь в Кураторий тайн

**Ягода:** Теперь мы ищем... «странного отшельника»?

**Нефер:** Но любое изменение не бывает беспричинным, будь оно вызвано внутренними причинами или внешними обстоятельствами.

**Нефер:** Кроме того, пропавший бродячий торговец тоже настораживает.

**Ягода:** Что? Но в тех местах полным-полно опасностей. Разбойники, чудовища... Да мало ли что.

**Нефер:** Если уж это общеизвестно, значит, торговцы, которые часто там ходят, должны иметь какие-то способы защиты.

**Нефер:** Тем не менее опытный торговец пропал бесследно. Должно быть, тут скрыта причина, о которой никто не знает.

**Нефер:** Ягода, на этот раз тебе предстоит навестить этого «странного отшельника».

**Нефер:** Путь дальний. Держись главной дороги.

**Ягода:** Хорошо, босс! Я не подведу.

### The Suspicious Hermit

In the northern Lempo Isle, east of the Nothing Passage, lived a hermit called Derwent who regularly visited friends in Nasha Town. Recently, however, not only has he stopped going to Nasha Town, he's also refusing all visiting guests. The recluse appears to have undergone significant changes in both appearance and habits... There is, however, another notable detail: a traveling merchant claims that their associate, who regularly delivered goods to the hermit, has gone missing after their last visit... To uncover the truth, a direct visit may be the best approach.

#### Visit the Curatorium of Secrets

**Jahoda:** So, this time we're investigating... a suspicious hermit?

**Jahoda:** Aren't hermits all suspicious by nature? Anyone who runs off to live halfway up a remote mountain can't be entirely normal...

**Nefer:** People don't change for no reason, regardless of whether those changes are driven by internal or external factors.

**Nefer:** Besides, that missing merchant raises questions...

**Jahoda:** Huh? But that area's full of dangers — everyone knows that. Bandits, monsters... There are a million ways a traveling merchant might meet their end out there.

**Nefer:** Seeing as everyone knows how dangerous it is, don't you think a merchant who regularly passes through would be well prepared?

**Nefer:** Despite knowing the lay of the land, an experienced merchant just disappeared. Something like that rightly raises suspicion.

**Nefer:** Jahoda, I'm leaving it to you to visit this "suspicious hermit."

**Nefer:** It's a long road — make sure you don't stray from the path.

**Jahoda:** Oh... Sure thing, Boss Nef! Leave it to me!

#### Investigate the hermit's hut

**Jahoda:** Hello? Is anybody there?

**Jahoda:** Mr. Derwent? Are you home?

**Derwent:** ...Who's there?

**Jahoda:** The name's Jahoda. I'm here from the Curatorium of Secrets. I was wondering if I could ask you a few questions...

**Derwent:** ...You can ask from there. I'm ill, don't expect me to get up for you.

**Jahoda:** (How strange... The Conch Gang kids said Mr. Derwent taught them how to read. They said he always spoke softly...)

**Jahoda:** (This voice is hoarse, but he doesn't sound ill. It sounds like his voice is naturally rough...)

**Jahoda:** Are you alright? If it's serious, I could help you find a doctor...

**Derwent:** ...No need. I'll be right in a bit.

**Jahoda:** Alright. In that case, you should head to Nasha Town when you're better — your friends have been worried about you.

**Derwent:** ...

**Jahoda:** (...Why's he not saying anything? I can hear someone walking around in there, but the footsteps are heavy...)

**Jahoda:** Have you not been outside much recently?

**Derwent:** ...I've been cultivating myself.

**Jahoda:** Yeah, word on the street is that you've recently had a bit of a makeover... Someone was saying they couldn't believe how strong you look — they said you used to look so thin when you were wearing robes.

**Jahoda:** And a mask to block out kuuvahki... Is that the latest fashion?

**Derwent:** ...It's to block out the noise.

**Jahoda:** So you can better cultivate yourself...?

**Derwent:** ...

**Jahoda:** Anyway, there was a merchant who came to visit you not long ago. Do you know where he went?

**Derwent:** ...No idea.

**Jahoda:** A—Alright, then... Sorry to bother you.

**Jahoda:** (I'm not sure why, but I feel it in my bones — this is getting dangerous...)

**Jahoda:** (They weren't wrong. This seems like a completely different guy...)

**Jahoda:** (I can't go back to Boss Nef having only learned so little. I'd better stay close and investigate the surrounding area.)

#### Look for clues outside the hut

**Jahoda:** (Why would he try to burn these...?)

**奈芙尔:** 另外，失踪的游商也令人在意。
